---
id: "1.4.3"
title: "Contrast (Minimum)"
level: AA
principle: perceivable
summary: Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background (3:1 for large text), so people with low vision can read it.
---
Low contrast text is one of the most common accessibility failures on the web, and one of the easiest to fix. Normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background; text that's at least 18pt (or 14pt bold) needs only 3:1.

This applies to real text — decorative text in a logo, or text that's part of an inactive/disabled control, is exempt. Tools like the WebAIM contrast checker or browser dev tools can measure the ratio directly from a hex color pair.
