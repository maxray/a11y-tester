---
id: "2.4.4"
title: "Link Purpose (In Context)"
level: A
principle: operable
summary: A link's purpose should be clear from its text alone (or its text plus surrounding context), so users know where it goes before activating it.
---
Screen reader users often navigate by pulling up a list of all links on a page, out of surrounding context. Vague link text like "click here," "read more," or "link" gives them nothing to go on in that list.

Link text should describe the destination or action on its own — "Download the 2024 annual report" instead of "click here to download."
