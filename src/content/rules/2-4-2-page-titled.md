---
id: "2.4.2"
title: Page Titled
level: A
principle: operable
summary: Every page needs a <title> that describes its topic or purpose, so users can identify and orient themselves — especially when navigating between tabs or pages.
---
The `<title>` element is the first thing a screen reader announces when a page loads, and it's what shows up in browser tabs, history, and bookmarks. A missing or generic title ("Untitled," "Home," or the same title on every page of a site) leaves users unable to tell pages apart or confirm navigation succeeded.

Titles should be unique per page and describe both the page's content and, usually, the site it belongs to (e.g. "Contact Us – Acme Co").
